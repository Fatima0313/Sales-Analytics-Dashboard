## Overall Finding

The analysis identified August 2024 as a significant period of sales decline compared with July 2024.

## Product-Level Findings

- The decline was observed across multiple products rather than being limited to a single product.
- Laptop and Tablet showed noticeable decreases in order volume and quantity from July to August 2024.
- Chair also experienced a decline in both orders and quantity.
- Desk and Monitor showed different patterns, demonstrating that product performance did not move uniformly.

## Important Observation

Printer showed an unusual pattern:

- Orders increased from 5 to 9.
- Quantity increased from 51 to 123.
- Average unit price decreased from approximately $347 to $268.
- Total sales increased substantially.

This shows that an increase or decrease in order count alone cannot explain overall sales performance.

## Business Interpretation

The analysis suggests that the August 2024 sales decline was influenced by changes in product-level demand and sales mix. However, the dataset does not contain enough information to establish the exact business reason for the decline.

Potential factors such as pricing, promotions, inventory availability, seasonality, or external market conditions would require additional data to confirm.

## Analytical Approach

The investigation followed this sequence:

1. Identified the month with the largest sales decline.
2. Compared July and August sales by product.
3. Compared order volumes by product.
4. Compared quantities sold by product.
5. Investigated unit price and discount patterns.
6. Identified unusual product-level behavior for further investigation.
